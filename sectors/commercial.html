Overview

Commercial property that delivers lasting returns

W13 Group’s commercial division acquires, develops and manages income-producing assets across the office, retail, industrial and logistics sectors. We take a long-term view, creating workspace environments that attract and retain high-quality tenants while delivering robust returns for our investment partners.

Our approach combines rigorous market analysis with hands-on asset management. We identify underperforming locations with strong fundamentals, add value through refurbishment or redevelopment, and stabilise assets for hold or disposal — tailoring our strategy to each opportunity.

With particular expertise in the West Midlands and Greater London markets, W13 Group is well positioned to capitalise on the structural shifts reshaping UK commercial property — from the growth of last-mile logistics to the evolution of hybrid workspace.

What We Deliver

Commercial capability across all asset classes

Office Development
Grade A and refurbished office space designed for modern occupiers, incorporating flexible floorplates, best-in-class specifications and ESG-compliant building performance.
Industrial & Logistics
Last-mile distribution, light industrial and logistics facilities positioned on strategic transport corridors. Purpose-built or refurbished to meet occupier requirements and EPC standards.
Retail & Leisure
Neighbourhood retail, convenience-led schemes and leisure facilities that serve local communities and generate stable, diversified income streams for our portfolio.
Asset Management
Proactive management of commercial portfolios including lease negotiations, tenant retention strategies, capital expenditure programmes and ESG improvement plans.
Refurbishment & Repositioning
Value-add refurbishment of existing commercial stock to improve rental tone, reduce voids and reposition assets within their local market context. Focus on EPC compliance and net-zero pathways.
Investment & Disposal
Structuring acquisitions and disposals of commercial property, working with institutional investors, private capital and family offices to deliver attractive risk-adjusted returns.
