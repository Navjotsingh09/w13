Overview

Regeneration rooted in place and community

Urban regeneration sits at the heart of W13 Group’s mission. We specialise in the complex, long-term work of transforming brownfield land, former industrial sites and underperforming urban areas into vibrant mixed-use neighbourhoods. Our approach brings together development expertise, planning knowledge and genuine community engagement to deliver lasting change.

We work closely with local authorities, Homes England, combined authorities and community stakeholders to align our schemes with local priorities. Whether supporting Levelling Up objectives, contributing to Local Plan housing targets or delivering biodiversity net gain, every project is shaped by the policy context and the needs of the people it will serve.

From initial site assembly and remediation through to placemaking and long-term stewardship, W13 Group takes a whole-lifecycle approach to regeneration. We don’t just build — we create places with identity, resilience and enduring value.

What We Deliver

Comprehensive regeneration capability

Brownfield Redevelopment
Bringing previously developed land back into productive use. We manage remediation, abnormal costs and environmental risk to unlock sites that others consider too complex or unviable.
Town Centre Renewal
Revitalising high streets and town centres through mixed-use development, public realm improvements and repurposing of vacant commercial stock. Aligning with local authority regeneration frameworks.
Estate Regeneration
Sensitive estate renewal delivering improved homes, better public spaces and stronger community infrastructure. We engage residents from day one and prioritise right of return for existing tenants.
Transport-Oriented Development
High-density, mixed-use schemes at railway stations, metro stops and bus interchanges. Maximising the connectivity premium to deliver sustainable, car-lite communities with strong public transport links.
Community & Placemaking
Embedding social value through community facilities, green infrastructure, meanwhile uses and public art. We create places with identity and a sense of belonging — not just housing numbers.
Public Sector Partnerships
Joint ventures and development agreements with local authorities, combined authorities and Homes England. We bring private investment and delivery capability to publicly-led regeneration priorities.
