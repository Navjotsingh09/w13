Architecture & Design

W13 leads in creating architectural designs across residential, commercial, education and healthcare sectors, renowned for our unwavering dedication to excellence. Our approach integrates advanced design technologies with a steadfast commitment to sustainability, ensuring that each project not only meets but exceeds the highest standards of quality. Our in-house studio combines creativity with commercial awareness — designing buildings that inspire while delivering on programme, budget, and planning objectives.

Key Capabilities

Concept Design

Transforming site opportunities and client briefs into compelling architectural visions through creative exploration, feasibility studies, and massing analysis.

BIM & 3D Visualisation

Full Building Information Modelling capability delivering coordinated design information, clash detection, and photorealistic visualisations for planning and marketing.

Detail Design & Specification

Comprehensive technical design packages and specifications that translate design intent into buildable, regulatory-compliant reality.

Sustainable Design

Integrating passive design strategies, renewable technologies, and sustainable materials to deliver energy-efficient buildings that exceed Part L requirements.

Interior Design

From residential show homes to commercial fit-outs — creating internal environments that enhance wellbeing, productivity, and desirability.

Planning Applications

Preparing and coordinating planning submissions including Design & Access Statements, heritage assessments, and pre-application strategies.

Our Approach

Our architectural practice sits at the heart of the W13 business. Having an in-house design capability means we can respond quickly to opportunities, control quality throughout the project lifecycle, and maintain a direct feedback loop between design intent and construction reality.

Context-responsive design

We begin every project with a thorough analysis of its physical and social context. Understanding the surrounding built environment, landscape character, and community needs ensures our designs enhance rather than disrupt the places they sit within.

Design quality through collaboration

Our architects work alongside our development, construction, and commercial teams from day one. This integrated approach means designs are commercially viable, buildable, and programme-efficient — without compromising on architectural quality or placemaking ambition.

From sketch to completion

We provide full architectural services from RIBA Stage 0 through to Stage 6, including post-occupancy evaluation. This continuity ensures design intent is maintained through construction and that lessons learned feed back into future projects.

"Great architecture doesn't happen in isolation. It comes from understanding the site, the community, the brief, and the commercial context — then finding the design that serves all four."
