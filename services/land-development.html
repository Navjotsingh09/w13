Our Services

Land & Development

W13 is renowned for its proficiency in end-to-end land and property development, from strategic land acquisition through to project completion. Our methodological approach ensures cooperation across every discipline, accelerates timelines, and simplifies communication — culminating in developments that create lasting value for communities and stakeholders. With over two decades of experience across the West Midlands and beyond, we have built a reputation for identifying opportunity where others see complexity.

Key Capabilities

Site Identification

Our land team proactively sources opportunities across the West Midlands, evaluating brownfield, greenfield, and strategic sites against rigorous viability criteria.

Land Acquisition

We structure acquisitions to suit every scenario — unconditional purchases, option agreements, promotion agreements, and joint ventures with landowners.

Planning & Consents

Our in-house planning expertise navigates the full consenting process, from pre-application engagement through to discharge of conditions.

Construction Delivery

Vertically integrated construction management ensures quality, programme, and cost are all controlled from within the W13 team.

Stakeholder Management

We build genuine relationships with local authorities, communities, and statutory consultees to smooth the development journey.

Financial Structuring

From appraisals and funding to exit strategies, our commercial team optimises returns while managing risk throughout the development cycle.

Our Approach

Our development methodology has been refined over 20 years and more than 500 projects. We operate across the full development lifecycle — acquiring land, securing planning, delivering construction, and managing disposal — giving us control over quality and programme at every stage.

Due diligence first

Every potential site undergoes rigorous assessment covering planning policy, ground conditions, environmental constraints, access, utilities, and market demand before we commit. This front-loaded approach means fewer surprises later.

Community-centred design

We design for the people who will live in and around our developments. Our in-house architecture team ensures that every scheme responds to its context — creating homes, streets, and spaces that enhance the existing neighbourhood.

Sustainable by default

From fabric-first energy strategies to biodiverse landscaping, sustainability is embedded in our approach from day one — not retrofitted as an afterthought.
