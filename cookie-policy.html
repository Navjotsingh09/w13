Cookie Policy

How and why we use cookies and similar technologies, and how to manage your preferences.

Effective date: June 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how W13UK ("we," "our," or "us") uses cookies and similar technologies when you visit and interact with our website. It should be read alongside our Privacy Policy and Terms & Conditions.

1. About This Cookie Policy

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, tablet, or other internet-enabled device) when you visit a website. Cookies help websites function efficiently, remember user preferences, improve performance, and provide insights into how visitors interact with the website.

Cookies may be set directly by us or by third-party service providers whose services are integrated into our website.

Why We Use Cookies

We use cookies to:

  • Ensure the website operates correctly and securely.
  • Remember your preferences and settings.
  • Improve website performance and user experience.
  • Understand how visitors use our website.
  • Measure the effectiveness of content and marketing activities.
  • Deliver relevant advertising where applicable.
  • Comply with applicable legal and regulatory requirements.

2. How We Use Cookies

First-Party vs. Third-Party Cookies

First-Party Cookies — These cookies are placed directly by W13UK and are generally used to enable core website functionality, maintain security, and remember user preferences.

Third-Party Cookies — These cookies are placed by external providers whose services we use, such as analytics platforms, advertising partners, embedded content providers, and social media services. These third parties may collect information in accordance with their own privacy policies.

Persistent vs. Session Cookies

Session Cookies — Session cookies are temporary and expire automatically when you close your browser.

Persistent Cookies — Persistent cookies remain on your device for a specified period or until manually deleted. They help remember your preferences and settings between visits.

3. Types of Cookies We Use

W13UK uses different categories of cookies and similar technologies to operate, secure, and improve our website. Some cookies are essential for the website to function, while others help us understand how visitors interact with our website and improve user experience.

4. Lawful Consent Framework (PECR & ICO Guidelines)

The "Consent" Mechanism

In accordance with the UK Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR), UK GDPR requirements, and guidance from the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), W13UK seeks user consent before placing any non-essential cookies on a visitor's device.

When you first visit our website, you will be presented with a cookie consent banner that allows you to:

  • Accept all cookies.
  • Reject non-essential cookies.
  • Customise cookie preferences by category.

Non-essential cookies will not be activated until valid consent has been obtained.

Implied vs. Explicit Consent

W13UK relies on explicit consent for non-essential cookies. Examples of explicit consent include clicking an "Accept" button or enabling specific cookie categories through preference settings.

We do not treat continued browsing, scrolling, or inactivity as valid consent where applicable laws require affirmative action. You may withdraw or modify consent at any time through our cookie preference management tools.

5. How to Control and Manage Cookies

Using Our Cookie Consent Management Banner

You can manage your cookie preferences at any time through our Cookie Preferences Centre, accessible from the website footer, the floating cookie settings button, or the cookie banner interface. You may accept all cookies, reject non-essential cookies, or enable or disable specific cookie categories. Changes to preferences will apply prospectively.

Browser-Level Cookie Settings and Controls

Most web browsers allow users to manage cookies through browser settings. You can generally delete existing cookies, block future cookies, receive alerts before cookies are stored, and restrict third-party cookies. Please note that disabling essential cookies may affect website functionality.

Opting Out of Targeted Advertising Networks

You may also manage advertising preferences through industry opt-out platforms and advertising network controls where available. Please note that opting out does not eliminate advertising entirely; it may simply make advertisements less relevant to your interests.

6. Updates to This Cookie Policy

W13UK periodically reviews and audits cookies and similar technologies deployed across the website to ensure ongoing compliance with applicable laws and regulatory guidance. Audits may be conducted following website updates or deployments, when introducing new technologies or third-party services, during compliance reviews, and at regular intervals throughout the year.

Where material changes are made to this Cookie Policy, we will update the "Effective Date" shown at the top of this page and take any additional steps required by applicable law.
