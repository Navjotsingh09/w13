UK Housing Market Outlook: Spring 2026 Forecast

The UK housing market enters Spring 2026 with renewed optimism. Mortgage rates stabilising, regional growth outpacing London, and the West Midlands emerging as a key growth market.

A Market in Transition

The UK housing market enters Spring 2026 with renewed optimism. After a period of adjustment following interest rate fluctuations, market fundamentals are strengthening — mortgage approvals are rising, buyer confidence is returning, and regional markets outside London are showing particular resilience.

For developers like W13 Group, these conditions present significant opportunity, particularly in the West Midlands where housing demand consistently outstrips supply.

Key Market Indicators

Mortgage rates stabilising

Average two-year fixed rates have settled around 4.2%, down from their 2023 peak. The Bank of England's measured approach to base rate adjustments has brought predictability that both lenders and buyers value. First-time buyer activity has responded positively, with Help to Buy legacy schemes and shared ownership continuing to support market entry.

Regional growth outpacing London

The West Midlands, North West, and East Midlands are leading price growth, with annual increases of 3-5% driven by strong employment markets, infrastructure investment (including HS2 related projects), and relative affordability compared to the South East.

Supply remains fundamental challenge

The UK continues to fall short of the widely cited 300,000 homes per year target. In 2025, approximately 220,000 new homes were delivered — an improvement on recent years but still leaving a cumulative deficit. This structural undersupply underpins long-term demand for quality residential development.

Implications for the West Midlands

Birmingham and the wider West Midlands region stand to benefit disproportionately from current trends:

  • Affordability premium: Average house prices remain 40-50% below London equivalents, attracting both homebuyers and institutional investors
  • Employment growth: The region's diversifying economy — spanning financial services, tech, and advanced manufacturing — is creating sustained housing demand
  • Infrastructure catalyst: Major transport investments are improving connectivity and unlocking development sites
  • Build-to-Rent expansion: Institutional appetite for BTR in Birmingham continues to grow, with the city now established as the UK's second-largest BTR market

"The fundamentals in the West Midlands are as strong as we've seen in two decades. Population growth, employment diversification, and infrastructure investment are creating a compelling case for residential development — both for owner-occupiers and the rental market."

W13 Group's Position

With over 20 years of experience developing across the region, W13 Group is well-positioned to capitalise on these market conditions. Our current pipeline includes residential, BTR, and mixed-use schemes across Birmingham, the Black Country, and surrounding areas.

Our approach — combining land acquisition expertise with in-house architecture and construction management — allows us to move quickly when opportunities arise, while maintaining the quality standards our communities expect.

Looking Ahead

The Spring market traditionally sees increased activity, and 2026 is shaping up to follow that pattern. We anticipate continued momentum through H1, supported by stable mortgage conditions and sustained demand in our core markets.

For landowners considering disposal, current market conditions offer strong residual land values. Our land team is actively seeking sites across the West Midlands.
