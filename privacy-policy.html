Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect your personal information.

Last updated: June 2026

Welcome to W13UK ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting and respecting your privacy. We ensure that your personal data is handled securely and in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018, and other applicable privacy laws.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit and interact with w13uk.com.

1. Important Information

Purpose of this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy aims to provide you with information about how W13UK collects and processes your personal data through your use of our website, including any data you may provide when contacting us, subscribing to communications, or using our services.

It is important that the personal data we hold about you is accurate and current. Please inform us if your personal information changes during your relationship with us.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers, technology platforms, analytics providers, cloud hosting providers, or software vendors may process personal data outside the United Kingdom. Where personal data is transferred outside the UK, we take appropriate steps to ensure that your information receives an adequate level of protection and is processed securely in accordance with UK data protection laws.

These safeguards may include the UK International Data Transfer Addendum, Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) approved by the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO), transfers to countries recognised as providing an adequate level of data protection, and other legally approved transfer mechanisms under UK GDPR.

Examples of providers that may involve international processing include website hosting providers, cloud service providers, customer relationship management systems, email service providers, and analytics services such as Google Analytics. We regularly review our international data transfer arrangements to ensure compliance with applicable data protection requirements.

7. Data Security

Technical and Organisational Security Measures

We have implemented appropriate security measures designed to prevent personal data from being accidentally lost, altered, disclosed, or accessed without authorisation. These measures may include SSL encryption, secure servers, access controls, firewall protection, and security monitoring systems.

Procedures for Suspected Personal Data Breaches

We have procedures in place to deal with suspected personal data breaches and will notify affected individuals and relevant regulators where legally required.

8. Data Retention

How Long We Retain Your Personal Data

We retain personal data only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes for which it was collected, including satisfying legal, regulatory, accounting, or reporting requirements.

Criteria Used to Determine Retention Periods

When determining retention periods, we consider the nature and sensitivity of the data, potential risks from unauthorised use or disclosure, legal obligations, business requirements, and applicable regulatory guidance. When personal data is no longer required, it will be securely deleted or anonymised.

9. Your Legal Rights (UK GDPR Chapter 3)

Under data protection law, you have the following rights regarding your personal data:

  • Right to Request Access — request a copy of the personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to Request Correction — request correction of incomplete or inaccurate personal data.
  • Right to Request Erasure — request deletion of your personal data where there is no lawful reason for continued processing.
  • Right to Object to Processing — object to processing based on legitimate interests or for direct marketing purposes.
  • Right to Request Restriction — request that we suspend processing of your personal data in certain circumstances.
  • Right to Request Data Portability — request transfer of your personal data in a structured, commonly used, machine-readable format.
  • Right to Withdraw Consent — where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time.

How to Exercise Your Rights

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us using the contact details provided above. We may request proof of identity before responding to your request.

Right to Lodge a Complaint with the ICO

If you believe your data protection rights have been violated, you have the right to lodge a complaint with the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ICO). For more information, visit ico.org.uk. We would, however, appreciate the opportunity to address your concerns before you approach the ICO.
