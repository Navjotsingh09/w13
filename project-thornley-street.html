Project Overview

A carefully designed residential development at Thornley Street

The Thornley Street project is a new-build residential development designed by W13 Group's in-house architectural team. The scheme delivers high-quality housing that responds to the character and context of the surrounding streetscape.

Our architects have developed detailed elevations for north, south, and east aspects of the development, ensuring each elevation presents a considered architectural response. The design balances contemporary construction methods with materials and proportions that complement the existing neighbourhood.

The ground floor layout has been optimised to maximise living space while maintaining generous circulation areas. This project exemplifies W13 Group's approach to delivering thoughtfully designed homes that enhance their local area.

Site Assessment

Conducted a thorough analysis of the Thornley Street site, assessing its potential for residential development and understanding the constraints and opportunities presented by the surrounding context.
