Our Story

Two decades of delivering landmark developments across the UK

Founded in 2004, W13 Group began with a clear vision: to transform underutilised land into thriving communities. From our first residential conversion in West London to managing multi-million pound mixed-use schemes, our trajectory has been defined by ambition grounded in meticulous execution.

Today we operate across the full development lifecycle — from site acquisition and planning through to construction, asset management and disposal. Our integrated model means we control quality at every stage, delivering projects that stand the test of time.

Headquartered in Birmingham with a project portfolio spanning London, the West Midlands and the North West, W13 Group has become a trusted partner for investors, local authorities and communities alike.

Sustainability

Lower carbon. Higher quality. Longer life.

Sustainability sits at the front of every W13 project, not as a tick box at the end of design. We prioritise brownfield regeneration, low carbon construction methods, fabric first energy performance, and biodiversity net gain across our sites.

Our specification favours durable, locally sourced materials wherever practical, and we design buildings to be efficient to run, easy to maintain, and adaptable over time.

We work openly with planning authorities, sustainability consultants, and our supply chain to make sure our environmental commitments are evidenced, deliverable, and aligned with current UK building standards.

Corporate Social Responsibility

Investing back into the communities we build in.

A development is more than a building. We work with local authorities and community organisations from the earliest stage of a scheme so that the people already living in an area benefit from what we deliver.

Our approach to social value includes local employment and apprenticeship opportunities through our construction partners, support for community spaces within our schemes, and contributions to local infrastructure where appropriate.

We also support a small number of charitable and community initiatives each year, focused on housing, education, and the wellbeing of the neighbourhoods we operate in.

Health & Safety

Zero compromise on
safety standards

Health and safety is embedded in every stage of our operations — from initial site appraisals through to project completion and handover. We maintain a proactive safety culture where every team member, subcontractor and visitor is empowered to identify and address risk.

Our dedicated H&S team conducts regular audits, toolbox talks and site inspections to ensure compliance with CDM 2015 regulations and industry best practice. We hold an exemplary safety record with zero reportable incidents across our most recent projects.
