Project Overview

A contemporary collection of family homes in the heart of Coseley

The Grange is a prestigious residential development comprising 24 high-specification family homes, ranging from three to five bedrooms. Delivered under a Design & Build contract, the project transformed a former brownfield site into a thriving new community.

Working closely with local planners and the community, W13 Group designed a scheme that complements the existing streetscape while introducing a modern architectural language. All 24 homes were sold within four months of launch — a testament to both the quality of build and the strength of demand in this popular West Midlands location.

Our Process

How We Delivered
The Grange

01

Land Acquisition & Due Diligence

Identified and secured a prime brownfield site in Coseley. Comprehensive due diligence included environmental assessments, title investigations, and viability studies to confirm the site's development potential.

02

Planning & Design

Our in-house design team developed a masterplan featuring 24 homes across a mix of 3, 4, and 5-bedroom configurations. Community consultation and pre-application engagement secured a smooth path through the planning process.

03

Pre-Construction

Detailed enabling works including site remediation, service diversions, and infrastructure installation. Value engineering workshops refined the specification while upholding build quality.

04

Construction

An 18-month build programme delivered through a phased approach, allowing early completions while construction continued on later plots. Rigorous quality management ensured NHBC standards were met throughout.

05

Completion & Handover

Phased handovers enabled buyers to move in ahead of schedule. Each home underwent a comprehensive snagging process and was presented to the highest standard. All 24 homes were sold within four months of launch.

06

Aftercare

A dedicated aftercare team provides ongoing support to homeowners. Our 12-month defect liability programme and responsive maintenance service ensure long-term satisfaction and peace of mind.
