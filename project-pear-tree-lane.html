Project Overview

A premium collection of bespoke family homes on Pear Tree Lane

Pear Tree Lane is one of W13 Group's most comprehensive residential developments, featuring a curated collection of individually designed house types. The development showcases the breadth of our in-house architectural capability, with ten distinct home designs ranging from the compact Coniston to the expansive Belmont.

Each home has been meticulously designed to respond to the site's character, with careful consideration of street scene elevations, landscaping, and the relationship between public and private spaces. The masterplan creates a cohesive neighbourhood where architectural variety sits harmoniously within a unified design language.

The project demonstrates W13 Group's end-to-end delivery capability — from site acquisition and planning through to construction and sales. With full architectural drawings, 3D renders, SketchUp modelling, and walkthrough animations produced in-house, Pear Tree Lane represents the gold standard of our design-led approach.

Our Process

How We Delivered
Pear Tree Lane

01

Site Acquisition & Feasibility

Identified a prime residential site on Pear Tree Lane with strong development potential. Comprehensive feasibility studies, including topographical surveys, environmental assessments, and market analysis confirmed the site's viability for a premium residential scheme.

02

Masterplanning & Design

Our in-house architectural team developed a comprehensive masterplan with ten distinct house types. The SketchUp site model allowed us to test massing, street scene relationships, and solar orientation before committing to detailed design.

03

Planning Application

Full planning application submitted with detailed Design & Access Statement, street scene elevations, and 3D renders. Pre-application consultation with the local authority and community engagement ensured a smooth planning process, achieving full approval.

04

Detailed Design & Specifications

Each of the ten house types was developed to full technical specification, with detailed floor plans, elevations, and construction drawings. Interior and exterior walkthroughs were produced to support marketing and sales efforts.

05

Construction & Delivery

Phased construction programme managed entirely in-house. Each plot is built to the highest specification, with rigorous quality control at every stage. The development showcases W13's ability to deliver complex multi-unit schemes on time and to budget.
