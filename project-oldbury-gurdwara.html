Project Overview

Conversion of Global House into a Sikh Gurdwara

The Oldbury Gurdwara project involves the conversion and extension of Global House into a fully functioning Sikh place of worship and community centre. This significant project transforms an existing commercial building into a facility that will serve the Sikh community for generations.

W13 Group's in-house architectural team developed the designs to meet the unique requirements of a Gurdwara, including spacious prayer halls, langar (community kitchen), and ancillary community spaces. The design respects traditional Sikh architectural principles while employing contemporary construction methods and materials.

The scheme includes careful consideration of the building's surroundings, with landscaping and parking designed to minimise impact on the local neighbourhood while creating a welcoming and accessible facility for the community.
