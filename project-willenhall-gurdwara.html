Project Overview

A new place of worship and community gathering for Willenhall

The Willenhall Gurdwara project represents W13 Group's commitment to delivering meaningful community buildings that serve local populations for generations. This new-build Sikh temple has been designed to provide a purpose-built place of worship alongside essential community facilities.

Working closely with the local congregation, our in-house architectural team developed designs that honour traditional Sikh architectural principles while meeting modern building standards and accessibility requirements. The proposed scheme features a generous prayer hall, community kitchen (langar hall), and ancillary spaces that support the congregation's diverse needs.

The project showcases W13 Group's capability in navigating complex planning requirements for community and religious buildings, ensuring the design respects both the cultural significance of the building and the character of the surrounding area.

Our Process

How We Delivered
Willenhall Gurdwara

01

Community Consultation

Engaged extensively with the local Sikh congregation to understand their needs for worship, community events, and daily use. This guided the design brief for a purpose-built Gurdwara with prayer hall, langar hall, and community spaces.

02

Architectural Design

Our in-house team designed a building that respects traditional Sikh architectural elements while incorporating modern construction methods and materials. Detailed floor plans, elevations, and 3D visualisations were produced to bring the congregation's vision to life.

03

Planning & Approvals

Prepared a comprehensive planning application including proposed views, floor plans, and builders works drawings. Navigated the planning process for this important community building, working with the local authority to secure approval.

04

Construction

With planning secured, W13 Group's construction team commenced on site. The build progresses with close attention to the specialist requirements of a Gurdwara, including the prayer hall acoustics, kitchen facilities, and ceremonial spaces.
